Your Move

Kooth

Mental health campaigns aimed at teenagers tend to sound the same, and teenagers tune them out. Your Move was Kooth's campaign to reach young people in schools and online with something that felt like it was made for them, not at them.

What I did

I set the creative strategy and built the campaign identity, then made the case internally for going much bolder than Kooth's usual look. Once that was agreed, I led the design and production work across print, digital, merchandise and in-school activations, keeping one clear idea running through every format.

The bold graphics and direct language were a deliberate bet: a health brand can be trusted and still feel like it belongs to the audience it's talking to.

Result

  • Rolled out nationally across schools, digital and social
  • Put printed materials and activations in front of tens of thousands of students
  • Gave Kooth a more confident voice with young people
