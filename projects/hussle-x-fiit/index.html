Hussle x Fiit

Hussle x Fiit

Fiit partnered with Hussle so members could train with the Fiit app in any Hussle gym. The launch needed to announce the partnership and, at the same time, push Fiit's bigger message: you can train with Fiit anywhere.

What I did

I came up with one simple, repeatable idea: a single continuous workout that moves between a Hussle gym, a living room and a park without breaking. It tells the partnership story and the brand story in the same shot.

I directed the hero film and planned the production so the same structure could be cut down for social and reused for future partnerships.

Result

  • Launched the partnership to both user bases
  • A creative framework Fiit could reuse beyond this launch
  • Reinforced Fiit's position as the fitness app you can use anywhere
