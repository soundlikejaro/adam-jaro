The Pretender

Kooth x The Diana Award

Kooth's own research found that one in three young people who are bullied don't tell anyone at home. For The Diana Award's annual anti-bullying campaign, we wanted to make that hidden experience impossible to ignore.

What I did

I led the campaign from the research insight through to launch. I wrote the script, set the creative direction across film, national digital OOH and press, and worked with The Diana Award's team so the campaign did a job for both organisations. I cast the lead through Sylvia Young Theatre School and directed the film with a small crew.

The brief I set was simple: no gimmicks. The story is built from real experiences young people shared with Kooth's practitioners, and the film stays on one face and lets it carry the weight.

Result

  • Winner, Brand Film Awards 2026
  • Featured on ITV, with coverage across national press
  • Ran nationally across digital OOH
