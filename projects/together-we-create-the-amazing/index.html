Royal Albert Hall America

Royal Albert Hall America raises money from US donors to support the Hall's work. They needed a film that would sit at the centre of their fundraising and make the case for long-term giving, without feeling like a sales pitch.

What I did

I started with the fundraising problem rather than the film. Working with the RAHA team, I shaped the narrative around what donors needed to feel before they'd commit, then wrote the script so the tiered giving opportunities sat inside the story rather than tacked on at the end.

I directed the production, combining new footage with archive from more than 150 years of the Hall's history.

Result

  • The flagship film for RAHA's US fundraising
  • A versatile asset for engaging high-value US donors
  • Presents the Hall as both historic and forward-looking
