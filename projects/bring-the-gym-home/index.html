Bring the gym home

Fiit

When the first lockdown hit, every home fitness app was suddenly chasing the same people. Fiit's CMO wanted national TV on air within weeks, while the moment was still there.

What I did

I wrote the concept and script and ran the project from brief to national broadcast in two weeks, on a £100k budget. That meant assembling the crew and deciding quickly what to cut to make the deadline without losing the idea.

The idea was as direct as the timeline: the gym, at home, now. It put Fiit's product and energy front and centre at the moment people were looking for exactly that.

Result

  • Aired nationally for over three months at the peak of lockdown demand
  • Contributed to a surge in sign-ups
