Unwind

MindLabs

At MindLabs, content was the product as well as the marketing. Most meditation content is interchangeable, so we wanted series that felt distinct enough to be a reason to choose the app.

What I did

I led the creative vision for two flagship series: Unwind (breathwork) and Five Senses (meditation). I worked with sound artists on bespoke soundscapes and directed shoots in remote locations, including a 300-year-old abandoned mine in Wales for Unwind.

The challenge was keeping ambitious productions simple and calming for the person using them, and making them repeatable enough to run as series alongside everything else my team was producing.

Result

  • Two flagship series in the MindLabs app
  • Gave the content library a distinct identity in a crowded category
