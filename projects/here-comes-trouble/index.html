Here comes trouble

Trouble Club

The Trouble Club is a community and talks organisation for women. Its events were full of energy, but its image needed modernising to appeal to a younger audience without losing the credibility it had built.

What I did

I shaped the campaign concept as part of a wider visual refresh: let the members sell it. We filmed at three of the club's busiest events over several months, capturing the atmosphere and interviewing members about what the club means to them.

I directed the shoots and led the edit into a set of campaign assets built for web, social and marketing.

Result

  • A refreshed, more energetic brand across the club's digital channels
  • Greater appeal to the younger audience the club wanted to reach
  • A flexible asset library the team could keep using
