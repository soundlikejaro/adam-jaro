London fashion week SS24

Emilia Wickstead

Emilia Wickstead wanted their SS24 London Fashion Week show to reach far beyond the people in the room, and to feel as considered on screen as it did on the runway.

What I did

As co-founder of Social Custard, I led our team through the project. We planned a multi-camera livestream from a historic London venue, ran setup and rehearsals over several days, and delivered the live show to thousands of viewers.

I set the plan for everything that came after the show too: long and short versions of the full show and a run of social edits for each platform, all agreed with Emilia Wickstead's team against their brand and the collection.

Result

  • Livestreamed to thousands beyond the live audience
  • A full suite of show films and social edits for Emilia Wickstead's channels
