MindLabs

MindLabs was a mental wellbeing app launching into a crowded, soft-focus category. As one of the first creative hires, I needed a launch idea that would make it look like the next generation of the category, and a creative engine that could keep feeding marketing after launch day.

What I did

I developed the launch concept: three real people, each using MindLabs in their daily life, shot to feel high-energy and contemporary rather than soft-focus. I directed the hero film and planned the shoot so it gave us far more than one ad.

That footage became the starting point for the performance creative. Cutdowns, variants and new hooks went into the weekly testing cycle my team ran against conversion data.

Result

  • Anchored the brand and app launch across every channel
  • Used extensively across paid social and performance campaigns
