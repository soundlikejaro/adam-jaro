Flauna

Live

Daily flora & fauna trivia

One species a day, the same for everyone. Five questions (what, where, who, when and why), then a result to share. Each question you answer adds a layer to a pixel-art woodland behind the game, until the scene is complete.

I designed and built all of it: the game, the animated scene, a small admin panel for scheduling species, and a backend that keeps tomorrow's answers hidden.

Built with · JavaScript · Cloudflare Workers · Claude Code

FM synth

In progress

A daily puzzle on a four-track FM synth

A pocket synth with four tracks: bass, keys, lead and drums. Every day there's a new puzzle, the same for everyone: a tempo and three rules about the shape, sound and placement of your pattern, checked live as you play. Or skip the puzzle, make something in free mode and share it with a link.

I designed and built the whole thing: the FM synth engine and its sound editor, a touch-first piano roll, the daily puzzles, and short share links that open straight into your pattern.

Built with · TypeScript · Web Audio · Cloudflare Workers · Claude Code

Clam Sync

In progress

Drop a folder, get a synced timeline

A Mac and Windows app for video editors. Drop in a shoot's footage and it lines up every camera and sound recorder by matching their audio, then exports a synced timeline for Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro or Avid. Clips are colour-coded by how they were placed, so you can see at a glance what to check.

I'm an editor, not a developer. I built it to replace PluralEyes on my own shoots, and it's grown into a full product, made for real shoots: hundreds of clips over several days, cameras with their clocks set wrong, and audio that drifts.

Built with · Python · FFmpeg · Qt · Claude Code
